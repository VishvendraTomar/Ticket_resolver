> **Demo data.** Northwind Global is a fictional company used to demonstrate Ticket Resolver. This is not legal advice. Statutory figures are summarised from the public sources cited under each clause (retrieved 2026-11-25); check the source before relying on them. Rules marked "Northwind rule" are invented company policy.

## 1. Annual leave (vacaciones)

1.1 Employees in Spain are entitled to paid annual leave of at least 30 calendar days (treinta días naturales) per year, under Article 38.1 of the Workers' Statute (Estatuto de los Trabajadores).

Source: https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2015-11430 (retrieved 2026-11-25)

1.2 Annual leave cannot be replaced by a payment.

Source: https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2015-11430 (retrieved 2026-11-25)

1.3 The dates are agreed between the employee and Northwind. Northwind rule: requests go through the HR portal.
