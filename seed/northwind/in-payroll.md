# Northwind Payroll Policy — India

> **Demo data.** Northwind Global is a fictional company used to demonstrate Ticket Resolver. This is not legal advice. Statutory figures are summarised from the public sources cited under each clause (retrieved 2026-11-25); check the source before relying on them. Rules marked "Northwind rule" are invented company policy.

## 1. Pay dates

1.1 Northwind pays employees in India monthly. Under section 17(1)(iv) of the Code on Wages, 2019, wages for a monthly wage period must be paid before the expiry of the seventh day of the succeeding month.

Source: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/15793/1/aA2019-29.pdf (retrieved 2026-11-25)

1.2 Northwind rule: salaries are paid on the last working day of each month, which is earlier than the legal deadline.
