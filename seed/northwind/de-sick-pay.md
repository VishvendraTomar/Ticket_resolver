# Northwind Sick Pay Policy — Germany

> **Demo data.** Northwind Global is a fictional company used to demonstrate Ticket Resolver. This is not legal advice. Statutory figures are summarised from the public sources cited under each clause (retrieved 2026-11-25); check the source before relying on them. Rules marked "Northwind rule" are invented company policy.

## 1. Continued pay during illness

1.1 Employees in Germany who are unable to work because of illness continue to receive their full pay from Northwind for up to six weeks.

Source: https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/krankengeld.html (retrieved 2026-11-25)

1.2 After six weeks, members of a statutory health insurance fund receive sickness benefit (Krankengeld) from their fund: 70 percent of regular gross pay up to the contribution ceiling, but no more than 90 percent of the last net pay.

Source: https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/krankengeld.html (retrieved 2026-11-25)

## 2. Reporting sickness

2.1 Tell your manager before the start of your working day. From the fourth day of illness, a medical certificate is required. Northwind rule.
