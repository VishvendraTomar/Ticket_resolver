# Northwind Notice Periods — United Kingdom

> **Demo data.** Northwind Global is a fictional company used to demonstrate Ticket Resolver. This is not legal advice. Statutory figures are summarised from the public sources cited under each clause (retrieved 2026-11-25); check the source before relying on them. Rules marked "Northwind rule" are invented company policy.

## 1. Notice from Northwind

1.1 The statutory minimum notice an employer must give is:

- at least one week if employed between one month and 2 years
- one week for each year if employed between 2 and 12 years
- 12 weeks if employed for 12 years or more

Source: https://www.gov.uk/redundant-your-rights/notice-periods (retrieved 2026-11-25)

1.2 Northwind gives at least one month's notice after the probation period, or the statutory minimum if that is longer. Northwind rule.

## 2. Notice from the employee

2.1 Employees give one month's notice after probation. Northwind rule.
