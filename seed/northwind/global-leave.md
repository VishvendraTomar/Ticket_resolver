# Northwind Global Leave Policy

> **Demo data.** Northwind Global is a fictional company used to demonstrate Ticket Resolver. This is not legal advice. Statutory figures are summarised from the public sources cited under each clause (retrieved 2026-11-25); check the source before relying on them. Rules marked "Northwind rule" are invented company policy.

## 1. Purpose and scope

1.1 This policy sets Northwind's annual leave rules for all employees worldwide. Where a country policy exists for an employee's work location, the country policy replaces this policy for that country.

1.2 Contractors are not covered by this policy.

## 2. Annual leave entitlement

2.1 Employees receive at least 25 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year, or the statutory minimum of their work location if that is higher. Northwind rule.

2.2 Leave accrues monthly from the start date and is pro-rated for part-time employees. Northwind rule.

## 3. Requesting leave

3.1 Leave must be requested in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for absences longer than three days. Northwind rule.

3.2 Unused leave may be carried over only as set out in Annex A.

## Annex A

Carry-over: up to five unused days may be carried into the next calendar year and must be taken by 31 March. Any other unused days lapse, unless local law requires otherwise. Northwind rule.
