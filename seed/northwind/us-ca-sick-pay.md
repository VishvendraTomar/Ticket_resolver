## 1. Paid sick leave

1.1 Since 1 January 2024, employees in California may use at least 40 hours or five days of paid sick leave per year, whichever is more.

Source: https://www.dir.ca.gov/dlse/paid_sick_leave.htm (retrieved 2026-11-25)

1.2 For example, an employee who works 10-hour days may use at least 50 hours of paid sick leave.

Source: https://www.dir.ca.gov/dlse/paid_sick_leave.htm (retrieved 2026-11-25)
