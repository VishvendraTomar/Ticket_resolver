## 1. Paid sick leave

1.1 New York State requires employers with 100 or more employees to provide up to 56 hours of paid sick leave per calendar year; employers with 5 to 99 employees must provide up to 40 hours.

Source: https://www.ny.gov/programs/new-york-paid-sick-leave (retrieved 2026-11-25)

1.2 Northwind has more than 100 employees, so employees in New York receive up to 56 hours of paid sick leave per calendar year.
