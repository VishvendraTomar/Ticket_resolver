# Northwind Payroll Policy — California

> **Demo data.** Northwind Global is a fictional company used to demonstrate Ticket Resolver. This is not legal advice. Statutory figures are summarised from the public sources cited under each clause (retrieved 2026-11-25); check the source before relying on them. Rules marked "Northwind rule" are invented company policy.

## 1. Paydays

1.1 Wages in California must, with some exceptions, be paid at least twice during each calendar month on regular paydays designated in advance.

Source: https://www.dir.ca.gov/dlse/faq_paydays.htm (retrieved 2026-11-25)

1.2 Wages earned between the 1st and 15th of a month must be paid no later than the 26th of that month. Northwind rule: Northwind pays on the 15th and the last working day of each month.

Source: https://www.dir.ca.gov/dlse/faq_paydays.htm (retrieved 2026-11-25)
