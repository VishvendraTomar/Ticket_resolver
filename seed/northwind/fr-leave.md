# Northwind Leave Policy — France

> **Demo data.** Northwind Global is a fictional company used to demonstrate Ticket Resolver. This is not legal advice. Statutory figures are summarised from the public sources cited under each clause (retrieved 2026-11-25); check the source before relying on them. Rules marked "Northwind rule" are invented company policy.

## 1. Annual leave (congés payés)

1.1 Employees in France earn 2.5 working days (jours ouvrables) of paid leave for each month of actual work with Northwind, whether full-time or part-time.

Source: https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F2258 (retrieved 2026-11-25)

1.2 This corresponds to 30 working days (jours ouvrables), or five weeks, for a full year of work.

Source: https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F2258 (retrieved 2026-11-25)

## 2. Requesting leave

2.1 Requests go through the HR portal; the main summer leave period is agreed with your manager by 31 March. Northwind rule.
