Northwind EU Leave Minimum

Demo data. Northwind Global is a fictional company used to demonstrate Ticket Resolver. This is not legal advice. Statutory figures are summarised from the public sources cited under each clause (retrieved 2026-11-25).

1. Minimum paid annual leave in the EU

1.1 Every employee working in a member state of the European Union is entitled to at least four weeks of paid annual leave per year under the EU Working Time Directive (2003/88/EC, Article 7). This minimum supplements Northwind's country policies: a country policy may give more, never less.

Source: https://europa.eu/youreurope/business/human-resources/working-hours-holiday-leave/working-hours/index_en.htm (retrieved 2026-11-25)

1.2 Paid annual leave may not be replaced by a payment in lieu, except when employment ends.

Source: https://europa.eu/youreurope/business/human-resources/working-hours-holiday-leave/working-hours/index_en.htm (retrieved 2026-11-25)
