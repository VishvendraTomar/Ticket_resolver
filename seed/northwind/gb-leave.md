# Northwind Leave Policy — United Kingdom

> **Demo data.** Northwind Global is a fictional company used to demonstrate Ticket Resolver. This is not legal advice. Statutory figures are summarised from the public sources cited under each clause (retrieved 2026-11-25); check the source before relying on them. Rules marked "Northwind rule" are invented company policy.

## 1. Holiday entitlement

1.1 Employees in the UK are entitled to 5.6 weeks of paid holiday a year. For someone working a five-day week, this is 28 days.

Source: https://www.gov.uk/holiday-entitlement-rights (retrieved 2026-11-25)

1.2 Bank holidays do not have to be given as paid leave, and an employer can choose to include them in the 28 days. Northwind rule: Northwind gives bank holidays in addition, so employees on a five-day week receive 28 days plus bank holidays.

Source: https://www.gov.uk/holiday-entitlement-rights (retrieved 2026-11-25)

1.3 Part-time employees receive 5.6 times their working days per week; for example, 3 days a week gives 16.8 days.

Source: https://www.gov.uk/holiday-entitlement-rights (retrieved 2026-11-25)
