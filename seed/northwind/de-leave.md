# Northwind Leave Policy — Germany

> **Demo data.** Northwind Global is a fictional company used to demonstrate Ticket Resolver. This is not legal advice. Statutory figures are summarised from the public sources cited under each clause (retrieved 2026-11-25); check the source before relying on them. Rules marked "Northwind rule" are invented company policy.

## 1. Scope

1.1 This policy applies to employees whose work location is Germany. It replaces the Northwind Global Leave Policy for Germany. The EU minimum continues to apply.

## 2. Annual leave

2.1 Employees in Germany receive 30 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year, based on a five-day week. Northwind rule.

2.2 The statutory minimum under the Federal Leave Act (BUrlG § 3) is 24 working days on a six-day week, which corresponds to 20 days on a five-day week. Northwind's 30 days include this minimum.

Source: https://www.bundestag.de/resource/blob/953308/08eb86111edc40fab10e41486a274855/WD-6-039-23-pdf-data.pdf (retrieved 2026-11-25)

2.3 The full entitlement is acquired after six months of employment; see section 3 for carry-over.

Source: https://www.bundestag.de/resource/blob/953308/08eb86111edc40fab10e41486a274855/WD-6-039-23-pdf-data.pdf (retrieved 2026-11-25)

## 3. Carry-over

3.1 Up to five unused days may be carried into the next calendar year and must be taken by 31 March. Northwind rule.

## Annex Munich office

The Munich office (Bavaria) closes on 24 December. Employees based there receive 24 December as an additional paid day off. Northwind rule.
