# Northwind Sick Pay Policy — United Kingdom

> **Demo data.** Northwind Global is a fictional company used to demonstrate Ticket Resolver. This is not legal advice. Statutory figures are summarised from the public sources cited under each clause (retrieved 2026-11-25); check the source before relying on them. Rules marked "Northwind rule" are invented company policy.

## 1. Statutory Sick Pay

1.1 Eligible employees who are too ill to work receive Statutory Sick Pay (SSP) of up to £123.25 per week, paid by Northwind for up to 28 weeks. The weekly rate is set by the government and changes each April.

Source: https://www.gov.uk/statutory-sick-pay (retrieved 2026-11-25)

## 2. Company sick pay

2.1 In addition to SSP, Northwind pays full salary for the first 10 working days of sickness absence in any rolling 12 months. Northwind rule.
